Privacy Policy

Hell Survivor · Effective October 5, 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Hell Survivor ("the game") uses. The game is made by an independent developer ("we").

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. We have no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of our own.

Your game progress (souls, unlocked characters, upgrades, scores, quests, achievements and settings) is saved only on your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Advertising

The game shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. Ads only play when you tap a button that offers a video. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • IP address and approximate location,
  • device model, operating system and app information,
  • ad interactions and diagnostic data.

This data is collected and handled by Google, not by us. See Google's policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice in the game's consent options or reset your advertising ID in your device settings. You can also opt out of personalized ads in Android settings (Settings › Google › Ads).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

Because we don't collect personal information, we don't store any on our side. Data processed by Google is protected under Google's own security practices.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date.
